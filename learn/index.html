How I teach

  1. 01

    We go slow.

    Nobody leaves a session feeling stupid. That has been the whole point of my tech help business, and it is the point here too.

  2. 02

    Plain language.

    I spent years turning sommelier jargon into something a normal reader would finish. AI jargon is easier.

  3. 03

    Real tasks only.

    We work on your actual emails, products, and problems, not toy demos you forget by Friday.

  4. 04

    No hype.

    Just the tools that work today, an honest view of what they cannot do, and what that means for your business.
