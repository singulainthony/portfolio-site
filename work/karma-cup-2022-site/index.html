The Karma Cup 2022 archive

An archive site for Toronto's Karma Cup cannabis competition, with official standings, category photo highlights, and the story of its final legacy year.

The Karma Cup was a Toronto cannabis competition and festival. The 2022 edition was its final legacy year, and this archive keeps it on the record: who competed, who won, and what it looked like.

Design

The look is warm and celebratory, not clinical. A cream and teal palette pulls from the trophy itself, with a chunky slab-serif headline and photos set on tilted cards like snapshots pinned to a board.

Content

  • Official standings for every category (Indica, Sativa, Hybrid, Solventless, Edibles, and more), presented as designed results boards.
  • Photo highlights: a compact grid of category entries in consistent rounded frames.
  • Story blocks that close out the competition and open the next chapter with the CANNA Lounge.

The archive is fully responsive, from phone to desktop.
