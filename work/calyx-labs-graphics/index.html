Graphic design

Calyx Labs product & lifestyle graphics

Product composites, lifestyle imagery, and technology graphics that gave a B2B hardware catalogue some personality.

Client
Calyx Labs, powered by CCELL®
Role
Marketing lead & designer
Year
2021–2022
Discipline
Graphic design
  • Compositing
  • Product graphics
  • Social

Vape hardware usually gets shot on white and listed by spec. That works for the spec sheet, but it doesn’t help a licensed producer imagine their customer holding it.

  • Lifestyle composites put each device in a real hand and a real moment, so the product pages felt like consumer brands rather than parts catalogues.
  • Product composites on marble and soft shadow gave hero products a premium context without a photo shoot.
  • Technology graphics like the glowing ceramic chip explained CCELL’s heating tech visually, before a buyer ever read a paragraph.
  • The sample box ties the line together into one brand moment for prospects.
