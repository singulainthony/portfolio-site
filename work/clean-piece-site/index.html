Web design

Clean Piece website & shop

The Squarespace site and online shop for Clean Piece, Toronto's first same-day bong cleaning service, which grew to over 10,000 pieces cleaned.

Client
Clean Piece (my business)
Role
Founder, designer, copywriter & builder
Year
2020–2022
Discipline
Web design
  • Squarespace
  • E-commerce
  • SEO
  • Copywriting

Clean Piece started from a simple, slightly gross truth: everyone knows cleaning glass is a chore, and nobody does it. So I started Toronto’s first same-day bong cleaning service, then added an online shop for cleaners and supplies.

The site

I built it on Squarespace and wrote every word, aiming for something funny, confident and a little cheeky in a category that is usually either clinical or stoner-cliché.

  • The hook up front: “You use cannabis to unwind. Clean Piece helps with the dirty work.”
  • Proof: over 10,000 pieces cleaned, before-and-after photos, and real reviews.
  • More than one revenue line: cleaning, glass repair, supplies, and white labelling for dispensaries, all from one site.
  • A cleaning-tips blog to bring in search traffic and point readers to the shop.

The site is no longer live. These screenshots come from the Wayback Machine’s November 2023 capture.
