Graphic design

Clean Piece brand identity

A crisp, clinical-but-friendly identity for Toronto's first bong cleaning service, from logo to packaging to before-and-after proof.

Client
Clean Piece (my business)
Role
Founder & designer
Year
2020–2022
Discipline
Graphic design
  • Logo design
  • Packaging
  • Photography

Cannabis branding usually goes one of two ways: green leaves and stoner jokes, or sterile medical. Clean Piece needed to feel like a service you’d trust with a $300 piece of glass: clean, competent, and still fun.

The system

  • The mark combines a lab flask and a bong inside a circular badge. It reads as “science-level clean” at a glance.
  • A wide, airy wordmark in all caps keeps it calm and premium.
  • Sky blue on white and near-black is the colour of clean water, deliberately not green.
  • Packaging: a simple white bag sealed with the icon sticker, so the brand shows up at the moment of handoff.
  • Before-and-after photography became the brand’s most persuasive asset on the site and on social.
