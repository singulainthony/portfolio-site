Graphic design

The Karma Cup identity & results boards

Logo, trophy art direction, and a system of results boards for Toronto's Karma Cup cannabis competition.

Client
The Karma Cup
Role
Graphic designer
Year
2022
Discipline
Graphic design
  • Logo design
  • Information design
  • Art direction

A competition lives or dies on two things: a trophy people want to win and results people trust. The identity had to carry both.

The mark

The logo is a bright yellow cup holding a triskelion (the karma symbol) next to heavy slab lettering. It’s bold enough for banners and merch, and simple enough to work as a single-colour stamp.

The trophy

The trophy is a sculpted glass piece. Shot on solid brand colours, it reads as an icon rather than a product photo, and those images anchor the website.

Results boards

Each category gets the same board system: a teal header, consistent columns for every judging criterion, and a clear total. Nine categories and dozens of entries stay readable side by side, so competitors can see exactly where they landed and why.
