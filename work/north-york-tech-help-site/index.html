North York Tech Help

A friendly, trust-first website for my in-home tech help business for seniors, written so adult children can confidently send their parents to it.

Most tech support sites are written for people who already understand tech. This one is written for a grandparent with an iPad and the adult child who’s tired of being the family IT department.

Decisions that matter for this audience

  • The headline sells relief, not services. “Get Tech Help Without Bothering Your Family” speaks to both the senior and their kids.
  • Calling comes first. A large call button sits above the fold and on every phone screen, because many clients would rather phone than fill in a form.
  • Large type and high contrast, with plain-language labels throughout. Nothing is hidden behind icons alone.
  • Trust is visible: a real face, real reviews, transparent pricing, a satisfaction guarantee, and a free follow-up.
  • Local SEO targets each North York neighbourhood I serve, so the site shows up when families search nearby.

I wrote the copy in the same plain, patient voice I use at the kitchen table.
