Calyx Labs website

When I joined Calyx Labs in late 2021, calyxlabs.com was a single “Under construction, check back soon” page. The company was selling CCELL vaporizer hardware to licensed producers across North America with no website to send them to.

What I built

I designed and built the site in Webflow. It covers the full CCELL catalogue (batteries, cartridges, pod systems, disposables and e-rigs), plus customization, technology, and a sample-request flow that gave the sales team somewhere to send every lead.

  • Structure for buyers. The site is organized around how B2B buyers shop: by format first, then by specs.
  • Brand voice. I wrote the copy, including “Technology that fits your needs” and the Calyx Promise, to make technical hardware feel approachable.
  • Product storytelling. Lifestyle and product graphics show the hardware in use, not just on a white background.
  • Search. I optimized every product page so licensed producers could find specific CCELL models.

There was no team to hand this off to, so the build, the copy, the graphics, and the reporting were all mine.

The site is still live. Some homepage banners have been updated since I left in late 2022.
